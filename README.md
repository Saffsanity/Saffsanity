## 🪟 Sill

[![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Saffsanity/sill?style=flat&logo=github&label=stars)](https://github.com/Saffsanity/sill/stargazers)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/Saffsanity/sill?style=flat)](https://github.com/Saffsanity/sill/blob/main/LICENSE)

[![App Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/App%20Store-Download-informational?style=flat&logo=apple&logoColor=white&color=000000)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6816359860)

**Any window from your Mac, live on your iPhone or iPad.** Touch, Apple Pencil, trackpad and keys. No servers, no accounts, Apache 2.0.

- Swift on macOS and iOS: ScreenCaptureKit capture, hardware HEVC encoding, TLS-paired local and remote transport, iPhone Duo layouts.
- Launched as the #1 Mac Streaming app on the App Store and #2 on Hacker News.
- 4.9 stars on the App Store (63 reviews), 859K channel views (18K likes), 35K+ iOS users and 38K+ Mac downloads.
- Community contributors have submitted Android, Windows, Linux and visionOS clients. Every pull request runs CI and mutation-tested checks; releases are tag-triggered, with TestFlight and App Store uploads from the command line.
- Built end to end with Claude Code: custom skills, workflows, MCP servers and artifacts for development, marketing, video and design. AI for iteration and prototyping, every architecture and product decision my own.

## 💼 Experience

**Microsoft** · Senior Software Engineer · New York, NY · 2019 – Present

- Designed the Azure-based infrastructure used in silicon manufacturing production for chip test across all generations of Maia and Cobalt first-party products.
- Built Azure DevOps CI/CD workflows with multiple build-validation pipelines on Azure cloud pools, plus version-control standards and documentation, for engineering teams totaling 100+ members.
- Generated and operated on many terabytes of manufacturing data across data centers and sites in the US, Malaysia, South Korea and Taiwan.
